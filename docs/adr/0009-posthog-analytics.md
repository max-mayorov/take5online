# PostHog for analytics and debugging, proxied through the Worker

Some problems only show up on other people's phones, and the Worker's logs can't show what the browser saw. PostHog (EU cloud, free tier) gives us exception tracking, session replays, pageviews and custom events from the app in one place. Visitors stay anonymous (there are no accounts, see [ADR-0003](./0003-no-accounts.md)); events carry the Room Code, Member id and Nickname so a report like "it froze in Room ABCD" can be found. The SDK talks to `/relay/*` on our own domain and the Worker forwards it to PostHog, because ad blockers commonly block PostHog's hosts. The SDK loads after the app, since it is about as big as the rest of the bundle. The costs: a third party sees how people play, and errors thrown before the SDK loads aren't captured.
